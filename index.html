05 · Assessment Protocol

MAKAUT Paper Format

Every core semester subject follows the official 70-mark university final examination structure, partitioned into Groups A, B, and C.

70 total marks
01
Group A
10 × 1 = 10 marks
Short definitions, multiple choice questions, truth/false verifications, and core formula applications across all syllabus modules.
02
Group B
3 × 5 = 15 marks
Medium analytical questions requiring clear diagrams, comparative tables, derivation steps, and concise technical explanations.
03
Group C
3 × 15 = 45 marks
In-depth design problems, numerical implementations, multi-part algorithmic derivations, and architectural case studies.
